KabelWacht is a minimal, fully free-software WireGuard® client for Android.

It does one thing well: manage WireGuard tunnels and connect.

Features:

* Create, list, edit and delete WireGuard profiles.
* Import a configuration by scanning a QR code.
* Import a configuration from a .conf file.
* One-tap connect/disconnect with a per-tunnel switch.
* Userspace WireGuard backend — no root required.

Privacy and freedom:

* 100% free and open-source software (AGPL-3.0-or-later).
* No trackers, no analytics, no advertising.
* No Google Play Services or other proprietary dependencies.
* The only network connection made is your own WireGuard tunnel.

Built on the official WireGuard tunnel library and the ZXing QR decoder.
