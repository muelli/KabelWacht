While a tunnel is connected, a status notification shows live traffic
and the last handshake, with a Disconnect button. The same numbers
appear on the connected tunnel's row; "no handshake yet" points at a
wrong key or an unreachable endpoint.

You are now alerted when a tunnel fails to connect unattended (run
conditions, always-on VPN) or is stopped by the system or another VPN.

Run-condition monitoring no longer stops itself right after starting.
