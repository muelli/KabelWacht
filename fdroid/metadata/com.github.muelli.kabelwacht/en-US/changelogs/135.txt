The edit screen now shows a tunnel's public key, ready to copy for
whoever administers the server.

Each home-screen widget can be pointed at a particular tunnel when you
place it, and resizes freely.

Run conditions are more dependable: a tunnel switched on by hand stays
on until the network changes, and automation and always-on VPN survive a
restart. On Android 13+ the app now asks for notification permission, so
its notices are no longer hidden.
