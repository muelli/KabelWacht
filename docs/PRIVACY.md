# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-31_

KabelWacht is a WireGuard VPN client. It is designed to collect **no** personal
data.

## What KabelWacht collects

**Nothing.** KabelWacht does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information or usage data. Specifically:

- **No analytics or telemetry.** There is no tracking, no crash reporting, and no
  usage statistics.
- **No accounts.** There is no sign-up, login, or cloud service.
- **No advertising.** There are no ads and no ad identifiers.
- **No third-party services.** The app contains no Google Play Services, no
  Firebase, and no other proprietary SDKs.

## Data that stays on your device

- **Tunnel configurations** (names, keys, addresses, endpoints) are stored only in
  the app's private storage on your device. They never leave your device except as
  part of the WireGuard tunnels you configure.
- **Camera** is used only, and only while you choose to scan a QR code, to read a
  WireGuard configuration. No images are stored or transmitted.

## Network connections

The only network connections KabelWacht makes are the **WireGuard tunnels you
configure and activate**. Where that traffic goes is determined entirely by your
own configuration and your VPN provider — not by KabelWacht.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published in this file in the project
repository.
